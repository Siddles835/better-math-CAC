# Translation review

These translations are AI-generated and have not been reviewed by a native speaker. Do not describe them as professionally translated. The English text is the source of truth. On Privacy, Cookie, and Support pages, the English version is the authoritative one.

## Worth a native-speaker review first

### zh-Hans

Kid-facing quiz stories in `quiz.json`, lesson prompts in `lessons.json` (counting apples, writing a number), and the speech phrases for plus, minus, and equals. Check that 八减三 sounds natural when read aloud.

### hi

Quiz stories, the minus phrase आठ में से तीन घटाओ, and Devanagari number-style labels. Math words such as जोड़ and घटाओ should stay simple for ages 5 to 8.

### es

Quiz stories and the equation-builder steps. Confirm Latin American wording is fine for a US classroom (`es-US` is the preferred speech tag).

### ar

Quiz stories, RTL layout around numbers, and the minus phrase ثمانية ناقص ثلاثة. Equations stay left to right on purpose, including 8 - 3 = ?. The solar-system map is not mirrored, because the orbit order is a spatial path from the Sun outward.

The orbit map stays visually unmirrored in Arabic so the Sun and the planet order children learn do not flip.
