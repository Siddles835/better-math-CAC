# Solar systems design

There is **one** live classroom solar system. Teachers choose the starting planet (Sun through Neptune). Students do not pick a pathway.

## Live — SS1 Foundations (`foundations`)

Counting → Addition → Subtraction across nine planets.

| Planet | Topic |
|--------|-------|
| Sun–Venus | Counting |
| Earth–Jupiter | Addition |
| Saturn–Neptune | Subtraction |

Topic starts: Sun, Earth, Saturn.

Extra pathway drafts (operations, place value, …) stay `comingSoon` and are not shown in the teacher or student UI.
